How much does a website cost?

Every business needs something different, so every project gets its own quote. Tell me about your business and goals, and I'll send you a custom proposal.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on how many pages you need and whether you also need branding and photos. I'll give you a clear timeline together with your quote.

Can you help my business show up on Google and in AI search results?

Yes. I set up and optimize your Google Business Profile, write your site so Google understands what you do and where, and add the behind-the-scenes business details search engines and AI answers rely on. No one can guarantee a top spot, but CG Aero now shows up first on Google for “Monterey aircraft maintenance.”

I don't have a logo or brand yet. Can you start from scratch?

Absolutely. Many of my clients start with nothing online. I can create your brand, website, Google presence and photos from the ground up.

Do you take photos too?

Yes. I do brand photoshoots, job-site photography, and product and event photos, so your website and Google profile show your real work.

Can you help after my website launches?

Yes. I offer ongoing care: site updates, fresh photos and content, Google profile management and regular check-ins on how you're showing up in search.
